How we make it

Four steps, from the field to the amber glass on the shelf.

Sourced

Certified organic growing partners, harvested at peak potency and traceable to the farm.

Extracted

Supercritical CO₂ extraction keeps the volatile compounds intact. No harsh solvents.

Tested

Every batch goes to an independent lab for purity, potency and heavy metals. Results on request.

Packed

Amber glass you keep, refill pouches you compost. Bring the jar back and we'll fill it.
