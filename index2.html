Darren has been counting tables

Before we sign a lease, we want to see people paying for good food on an ordinary weekday. So Darren, our director, has been sitting outside busy cafes and counting.

Outside Bonne Bouche on King Street, every table was full by 11am.

Where we might open

We’ve ranked eight towns on how busy they are all year round, not just in the summer holidays.

Witney

Excellent

About 31,000 people. The biggest town on our list.

Cirencester

Strong

About 20,000 people. The biggest town in the Cotswolds itself.

Stroud

Strong

A busy farmers’ market, and one of The Times’ best places to live for 2026.

Tetbury

Medium

A lovely high street, but fewer people live there.

Stow-on-the-Wold

Low

Packed in summer, quiet in winter.

Where the food comes from

  1. Grown

    On organic farms, most of them close enough to visit in a morning.

  2. Pressed

    Juice made in small batches, with nothing added.

  3. Tested

    Every batch can be traced, and we’ll send samples off for independent testing.

  4. Refilled

    Bring back your glass bottles and tea jars and we’ll fill them up again.

Suppliers in our plan: Abbey Home Farm, Fresh Direct, The Community Farm and Roots & Fruits.

What the shop will be like

A juice bar at the front, tables in the middle and food shelves at the back. Coffee on the way to work, lunch, then milk and bread on the way home.
